# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ChronosTrace community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability,
personal appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

## Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and technical critiques.
- Focus on what is best for the reliability, safety, and usability of
  the project.
- Never publish other people's private information, credentials, or
  unscrubbed recordings.
- Give credit fairly and do not present other people's work as your own.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and the publication of
private information are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive behavior
after a warning is also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful,
which may include warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal
from project spaces.

Reports of unacceptable behavior should be made privately to the
maintainers rather than through public issues.
